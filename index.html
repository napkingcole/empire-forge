If you can read AoE2's tech tree and know what a civ bonus does, you have all the expertise this tool requires.

Point, click, play

Every bonus, unit and tech is a plain-language card or a click on the tech tree — never a cryptic effect command. Empire Forge handles the game's data file for you.

Runs on your PC

No account, no upload, no website to go down. The app reads your own game files, builds the mod on your machine, and keeps working even if nobody maintains it.

Survives every DLC

Nothing is baked in from an old copy of the game. Each build reads your installed version, so new civs and units don't break your mods.

Share with friends

Save civs as small files, trade them, and combine several into one mod. Everyone in the lobby just needs the same mod enabled.

Three steps

From idea to skirmish in minutes

The full walkthrough is on the How It Works page.

1

Design your civ

Step through the wizard: identity, bonuses, team bonus, tech tree, unique unit, unique techs and heroes. Save it as a .civbuilder.json file.

2

Build the mod

Upload one or more civs, choose which original civ each one replaces, and click Build. You get a zip a few seconds later.

3

Play

Unzip both mod folders into your local mods folder, enable them in the game, and start a skirmish.
