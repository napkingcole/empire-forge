Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: Empire Forge runs on your computer and collects nothing about you.

Effective 7 October 2026. This policy covers the Empire Forge app (the GitHub download and the Microsoft Store version) and this website. Empire Forge is a free, open-source hobby project by NapKingCole.

What we collect

Nothing. Empire Forge has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app, and never sees your civs or your game files.

What the app does on your computer

The app runs a small local web server that only your own computer can reach (127.0.0.1) and shows its pages in your browser. While it runs, it:

  • Reads your Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition game files to build mods. It never modifies them.
  • Saves your custom bonus library and some caches that make builds faster in %APPDATA%\EmpireForge, and temporary build files in your Windows temp folder.
  • Keeps civs in progress in your browser's local storage for that page, so a refresh doesn't lose your work.

All of this stays on your computer. You can delete it at any time: remove the EmpireForge folder, clear the site data for 127.0.0.1 in your browser, or uninstall the Microsoft Store version.

Network requests

Empire Forge never sends your civs, mods or game files anywhere. It does make a few ordinary web requests, and the services that answer them see what any website sees — your IP address and basic browser information — under their own privacy policies:

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare).

Contacting us

If you email us or post on Discord or GitHub, we see what you choose to send, and use it only to reply and fix problems. We don't sell or share it.

Children

Empire Forge collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. The source code for both the app and this site is public, so any change to what the app does is visible on GitHub.
